## Design Specificity Verdict

**This is not an AI-generated design, and the honest answer to "identify AI issues" is that the AI problem here is not the look — it is the drift between what the project documents and what it ships.**

The authored core is genuinely distinctive and would be hard for another product to wear unchanged:

- `globals.css` is real design work: oklch tokens, one warm accent hue, `--measure: 660px` justified as ~70 characters of Newsreader at 19px, a redefined near-square radius scale (`rounded-lg` = 3px), a collapsed shadow scale because "almost nothing on this site floats", and a genuine `prefers-reduced-motion` guard at `:241`.
- Light-mode `--tx3` carries a comment documenting a measured 4.27:1 AA failure and the exact correction — that is craft, not generation.
- The home page carries `"No hero image, no feature cards"` as an explicit stance (`page.tsx:15`) and honors it: real sponsors, real bylines, four real mechanism photographs, and the syllabus as the centerpiece rather than a footer link.
- `Box.tsx:29-46` documents _why_ tinted cards were abandoned: "On a page with six of them the colour stopped carrying information and started reading as decoration, which is exactly how a real warning gets skipped." That is a point of view.
- The colour-scale sweep CLAUDE.md claims actually succeeded — **1 hit sitewide**, and it is inside a CSS comment (`globals.css:459`).

**The failure is that this system stops at the shell, the home page, and the token file.** Behind it sit 26k lines of hand-written lesson TSX that predate or ignore it:

1. **The documented lesson vocabulary is dead code.** CLAUDE.md presents `lesson/Prose.tsx` as the thing that "keeps 29 pages looking like one site." The only occurrences of `<Prose>`, `<Split>`, `<MarginNote>`, `<WatchOut>` in the entire repository are inside **that file's own doc comment** (`Prose.tsx:7-10`). Zero real usages. `ProseBlock` has none anywhere. `Mark` is used once, inside `KeyConceptSection`. And the file shows as **modified in your working tree** — it was edited this session while being dead.
2. **Three component files are fully unreferenced**: `Footer.tsx`, `LessonBreadcrumb.tsx`, `PageHero.tsx` — all still described in CLAUDE.md's architecture list. `PageHero.tsx:57` is itself flagged by the detector for a `border-l-2` side-tab.
3. **`/planner` was never brought into the redesign.** 230 legacy-alias token references (`--background`, `--card`, `--border`) vs 40 design tokens, generic `rounded-md` + `focus:ring-2` shadcn-dialect inputs, and no `<h1>`. Every other surface uses zero legacy tokens. It reads as a different product.
4. **`prerequisites/page.tsx` is a pre-redesign artifact in the worst possible slot** — lesson 02, where a student hits their first real friction. Seven `border-l-4 border-[var(--accent)] pl-4` side-tabs, an 8×8 Lucide icon jammed inside an `<h2>`, and `ContentCard` (a component surviving on 4 pages).
5. **There is no type or spacing scale.** 25 distinct hand-set `fontSize` values (7.5, 9, 9.5, 10, 11, 11.5, 12, 13, 14, 14.5, 15, 15.5, 16, 16.5, 17, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 25, 26, 27, 29…) across **461 inline `style={{}}` blocks** (273 in pages, 188 in components). The colour layer is tokenized; the typography layer is 461 independent decisions. This is why drift is inevitable rather than accidental — nothing can enforce anything.
6. **The project's own design brief was LLM boilerplate.** `context/design-principles.md` was a verbatim _"S-Tier SaaS Dashboard Design Checklist (Inspired by Stripe, Airbnb, Linear)"_ prescribing a persistent left sidebar, moderation queues, avatars, and zebra-striped admin tables — for a 29-lesson reading site that explicitly deleted its sidebar. `context/style-guide.md` documented the `--color-primary-*` / `--color-slate-*` scales that were later swept out entirely. Both described a product this never was.
7. **That governance is now dangling.** All of `context/` is deleted, yet CLAUDE.md:234-244 still instructs every future agent to "Compare against `/context/design-principles.md` and `/context/style-guide.md`", and CLAUDE.md:145 still says to read `context/ia-audit.md` before moving lessons. Next.js is on 16.2.12; CLAUDE.md says 15.4.6.

## Overall Impression

The bones are better than the body. Someone with real taste built the frame — the token file, the shell, and the home page are work I would show as an example of restraint done right, and the comments prove the reasoning was actually had, not generated. Then 29 lesson pages were filled in by hand, 26k lines of them, without that system being enforceable, and the two halves have drifted apart.

**The single biggest opportunity**: the lesson body is composed of interruptions. 297 asides at ~10 a page means the page has no argument, just a stack of boxes — and the components written to fix exactly that (`Prose`, `Split`, `MarginNote`, `WatchOut`) are sitting unused in a file whose own comment explains why they exist. Adopting them is not new design work. It is shipping design work that is already written and already paid for.

## What's Working

1. **The token file is a genuine design document.** `globals.css` explains _why_ at every decision — the 660px measure as 70 characters, the 4.27:1 contrast correction on one theme only, why code stays dark in light mode, why `min-width: 0` is load-bearing. Most projects have tokens; this has reasoning.
2. **The home page refuses the obvious.** "No hero image, no feature cards" is honored. Putting the syllabus at the center because "the hardest thing about a 29-lesson course is believing it has a shape" is a real insight, and the four mechanism photographs are the pitch precisely because they are real.
3. **`Box`'s reform was the right call, correctly argued.** Replacing six tinted washes with one mono label plus a rule, keeping colour for `danger` only, and accepting-then-ignoring the legacy `icon` prop is exactly how to retire a pattern without a 297-site sweep.
